anatomy.glb
-----------
The 3D shapes used by the digital twin: skin, skeleton, heart with its main
vessels, lungs with the airway, and brain.

Source:  BodyParts3D, release 3.0, obtained through
         https://github.com/Kevin-Mattheus-Moerman/BodyParts3D

licence. The licence covers this model file only, not the rest of the
project's code.

Changes made for this project: the parts for each group were merged; only the
outward-facing skin surface was kept; the lung artery was trimmed to the part
beside the heart; every group was simplified to far fewer triangles and lightly
smoothed; the model was turned to face the viewer, scaled to 1.75 m tall with
the feet on the floor, and saved in the glTF binary (.glb) format.
