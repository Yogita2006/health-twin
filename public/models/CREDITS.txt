body.glb
--------
The human body shape used by the 3D digital twin.

Source:  the MakeHuman base mesh (file makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj)
         https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
Licence: CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication), as stated in the
         MakeHuman repository file LICENSE.ASSETS.md

Changes made for this project: only the "body" part of the mesh was kept,
it was scaled to 1.75 m tall with the feet on the floor, the arms were lowered
to a relaxed pose, and it was saved in the glTF binary (.glb) format.
